Why we exist

Technical education was underbuilt. That is fixable.

Africa's technical workforce is enormous in number and underserved in quality. Young people enter the trade economy without structure, without credentials, and without the professional formation that turns skill into a career.

The result is a cycle that has nothing to do with the ability of the people in it, and everything to do with the absence of the right institutions. Kalvia exists to break that cycle.
